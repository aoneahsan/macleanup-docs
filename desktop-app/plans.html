Plans and allowance

Previews are free on every plan, without a limit. Plans only count real runs — runs that change files. Previews, reports and the disk gauge never count. The command line, npx macleanup, is MIT licensed and has no limits.

Every plan has all 28 sections and every safety check.

Free in detail​

A real run uses one of 15 a month on that Mac. Signing in with Google adds 15 more and keeps your run numbers across your Macs. Signing in is optional.

Joining a team​

A team owner invites you by email. Sign in to the desktop app with the Google account that has that email, open Team, and choose Accept the invite (app 5.3.1 or later). Your seat includes Pro. Joining adds this Mac's run numbers to the team's fleet report; file paths stay on this Mac. An invite expires after 14 days, and it cannot be accepted when every seat is in use.

Paying and cancelling​

Checkout opens in your browser through Polar, the payment provider. You can cancel any time; your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then moves to Free.

For billing questions, see the Terms of Service.
