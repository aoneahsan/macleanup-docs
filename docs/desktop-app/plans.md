# Plans and allowance

**Previews are free on every plan, without a limit.** Plans only count real
runs — runs that change files. Previews, reports and the disk gauge never
count. The command line, `npx macleanup`, is MIT licensed and has no limits.

Every plan has all 28 sections and every safety check.

## The three plans {#plans}

Prices as of 2026-10-30.

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $4 a month or $36 a year | $3 a seat a month or $30 a seat a year, from 3 seats |
| Real runs a month | 15 on each Mac, 15 more after Google sign-in | No limit | No limit |
| Previews and reports | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Receipts on your Mac | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Synced history | — | Up to 5 Macs | Up to 5 Macs a seat |
| Scheduled previews and safe-batch runs | — | Yes | Yes |
| Multi-Mac reports and CSV exports | — | Yes | Yes |
| Shared fleet report | — | — | Yes, across the team's Macs |
| Invite and remove members | — | — | Yes |

A Team fleet report carries run numbers only; paths stay on each Mac. See
[Privacy in the app](/desktop-app/privacy).

## Free in detail {#free}

A real run uses one of 15 a month on that Mac. Signing in with Google adds 15
more and keeps your run numbers across your Macs. Signing in is optional.

## Paying and cancelling {#billing}

Checkout opens in your browser through Polar, the payment provider. You can
cancel any time; your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then
moves to Free.

For billing questions, see the
[Terms of Service](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/terms).
