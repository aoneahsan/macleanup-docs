# What a white-label build delivers

Each group is the macleanup you can download today, renamed for you. The
sections, profiles and safety rules stay the same; see
[What gets deleted](/before-you-run/what-gets-deleted) before you ship it.

## The desktop app {#desktop}

- Your app name, icon and colours in the window, the menu bar and the disk image.
- One disk image for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later.
- Its own signed update feed, so each macleanup release reaches your users
  through the app's updater.
- A signed and notarised build needs an Apple developer account in your name.
  macleanup itself is not signed with an Apple developer certificate yet; see
  [Install and first launch](/desktop-app/install).
- Which account features your build switches on (sign-in, plans, the
  assistant), and whose services they run on, is agreed before we build.

## The command-line tool {#package}

- Published to npm under your package name or scope, runnable with `npx`.
- The same 28 sections, profiles, `--dry-run` and deep-section flags.
- Help text and readme in your name.

## From payment to your first build {#timeline}

1. **Choose and pay** on the [pricing page](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing),
   by bank transfer to our Wise account. Once we have confirmed the transfer,
   the licence key and certificate arrive by email.
2. **Send your brand.** Reply with your name, icon, colours, npm scope and the
   site your download page lives on. We answer with a date for the first build.
3. **We build and publish.** Your disk image and package ship under your name.
   Each deployment carries its licence file, so anyone can
   [check it](/white-label/verify).

Changes beyond your name, icon and colours are
[custom work](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted first.
