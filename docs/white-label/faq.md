# White-label questions

## Is a licence exclusive? {#exclusive}

No. We may license macleanup to other buyers until someone buys out the
desktop app. After that buyout we stop selling macleanup white-label licences.

## Do I get the source code? {#source}

The command-line tool's code is already public under MIT. The desktop app's
code is private and changes hands only with a
[buyout](/white-label/overview#buyout).

## Can you change more than the name? {#changes}

Name, icon and colours are part of the licence. Anything else is
[custom work](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted
before we start.

## Which costs are mine? {#costs}

Services you choose: your domain, an Apple developer account, an npm
organisation and any paid service your build uses.

## Where are the full terms? {#terms}

The [licence terms](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms)
in plain words, and the payment terms in the
[Terms of Service](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/terms#t-white-label).
