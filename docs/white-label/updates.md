# Updates and renewals

An update is a macleanup release rebuilt under your name: changes to what a
section removes or skips, fixes to the safety flags, macOS path and permission
changes, and the desktop app's own changes. The desktop build receives it
through its update feed; the package is republished under your npm name.

Every licence includes a period of updates from the purchase date. The length
and the renewal prices are on the
[updates page](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates).

## Renewing {#renew}

Renew on the [updates page](https://macleanup.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates)
with your licence key and the email on the licence. Renewals come in 3, 6 or
12 months. The months start from today or from your current end date,
whichever is later.

Without a renewal, your build keeps running on the last version we shipped.
